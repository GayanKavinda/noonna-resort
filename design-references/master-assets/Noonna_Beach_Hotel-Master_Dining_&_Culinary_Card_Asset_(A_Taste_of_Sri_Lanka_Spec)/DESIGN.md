## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a luminous, high-end sanctuary aesthetic tailored for private coastal hospitality on Sri Lanka’s southern shoreline. The identity balances effortless serenity with refined architectural structure. 

The aesthetic blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile & Fluid Coastal Influences**:
- **Atmosphere:** Sun-drenched, airy, untroubled, and organic. Pure white architectural planes framed by Indian Ocean horizon blues.
- **Audience:** Discerning global travelers, design-conscious retreat seekers, surf connoisseurs, and luxury vacationers desiring unpretentious barefoot elegance.
- **Emotional Response:** Instant decompression, gentle maritime calm, tactile warmth, and uncompromising clarity.

The visual rules demand spacious breathing room, disciplined monochromatic blues against pristine white, airy card compositions, and fine editorial typography contrasting with crisp functional interface chrome. Strict omission of heavy saturated tropical oranges, neon teals, or dark mode alternatives ensures the interface feels permanently bathed in clean tropical midday daylight.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated for a clean, light-drenched coastal environment. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Surface / Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominates 70% of the visual plane, functioning as high-noon sunlight hitting limewashed walls.
- **Alternating Tint / Canvas Muted (`#F1F9FD` Cloud Blue):** Provides subtle, restorative shifts in section backgrounds and low-contrast surface grouping.
- **Structural Outlines & Dividers (`#D9EAF3` Sea Mist):** Delicate, architectural borders and bento tile boundaries that retain airy definition without visual weight.
- **Subtle Accents & Badges (`#B8E0F5` Sky Blue):** Light maritime wash for tags, pill backgrounds, selection states, and micro-delighters.
- **Interactive Primary (`#397FA8` Ocean Blue):** Grounded maritime azure reserved strictly for focal primary CTAs, active indicators, tab underlines, and key interactive moments.
- **Deep Coastal Text & Display (`#173F56` Deep Coastal Blue):** Replaces harsh standard blacks with deep oceanic slate, providing optimal AA/AAA contrast ratios for high-end typography and line icons.

## Typography

The typography system bridges high-editorial luxury with hyper-functional, modern digital utility:

- **Display & Headlines (EB Garamond):** Brings classical warmth, literary rhythm, and relaxed seaside stateliness. Used for hero titles, room names, editorial storytelling, and pricing numerals.
- **Interface & Content (Inter):** Neutral, crisp, and highly legible across dense booking engines, operational metadata, amenity badges, and interactive controls.
- **Editorial Script Accents:** Reserved strictly for short micro-phrases (e.g., *"by the ocean"*, *"morning surf tide"*, *"private reserve"*) formatted in a flowing script like Moon Dance or italicized Garamond display to introduce authentic coastal charm without impeding legibility.

Headings over 32px automatically compress via mobile-specific tokens to prevent awkward hyphenation on narrow viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors modern coastal architecture: expansive visual breezeways, crisp grid alignments, and asymmetric bento modules.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer canvas margins (minimum 64px on wide screens). Supports organic bento modules spanning columns (e.g., 8+4 hero room overview, 4+4+4 experience highlights).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Bento modules gracefully consolidate into balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 16-20px side margins. Bento cards reflow vertically with maintained internal padding.

Vertical rhythm relies on generous intervals (80px to 120px between sections) to evoke the slow, restorative pace of the ocean.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the radiant, sunlit presence, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows, synthetic dark blurs, and harsh borders:

1. **Ambient Coastal Luminance:** Shadows are ultra-diffuse, tinted with subtle deep ocean pigments rather than charcoal.
   - *Resting card shadow:* `0 4px 24px -4px rgba(23, 63, 86, 0.05)`
   - *Hover / Modal shadow:* `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(23, 63, 86, 0.09)`
2. **Ghost Perimeter Outlines:** Bento cards and containers utilize a delicate 1px border in `#D9EAF3` (Sea Mist) paired with a pure `#FFFFFF` background.
3. **Layered Cloud Planes:** Depth is achieved by placing pure `#FFFFFF` cards over large `#F1F9FD` (Cloud Blue) backdrop bands rather than relying on shadow stacking.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects ocean-weathered rocks, softened coral edges, and clean modern tropical concrete work:

- **Hero & Primary Visual Containers:** Large, sculptural radius from 20px to 28px (`rounded-xl` / custom hero wrappers), framing photography of surf breaks, private villas, and dining pavilions.
- **Bento Tiles & Content Cards:** 16px to 24px corners (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), creating inviting, soft structural units.
- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs):** 8px to 12px corners (`rounded-md`), preserving precise tactile utility without turning fully pill-shaped.
- **Status Badges & Category Tags:** Fully circular / pill-shaped for quick visual distinction against structural layout cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Ocean Blue (`#397FA8`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text in `Inter` SemiBold (14px). Subtly scales luminance on hover (`#2E688A`). 8px to 12px border radius. No heavy bevels or gradients.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#397FA8`, text in `#397FA8`. Hover fills with 8% opacity Cloud Blue (`#F1F9FD`).
- **Tertiary / Link:** Deep Coastal Blue (`#173F56`) with a fine 1px underline offset by 4px, accompanied by an ultra-light chevron icon.

### Bento Tiles & Cards
- Built with a `#FFFFFF` core, subtle 1px `#D9EAF3` border, and 20px padding. 
- Integrated imagery features soft 12px internal radii, maintaining internal layout padding.
- Micro-hover interactions subtly elevate the card with a soft maritime shadow and an ambient `+2px` translateY transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Amenity / Tag Chip:** Cloud Blue (`#F1F9FD`) surface, subtle `#D9EAF3` border, `#173F56` body-sm text, pill-shaped (9999px radius).
- **Featured / Status Badge:** Sky Blue (`#B8E0F5`) surface with Ocean Blue (`#397FA8`) bold micro-typography (`label-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Input Fields:** Crisp 48px height, pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#D9EAF3` border, 8px radius. Active focus state swaps border to `#397FA8` with a soft 3px `#B8E0F5` glow.
- **Booking Bar:** A unified floating horizontal surface with discrete vertical dividing rules in Sea Mist (`#D9EAF3`), grouping Check-in, Check-out, Guests, and CTA into a seamless island.

### Iconography
- Clean, thin-line Lucide-style outline icons rendered with a consistent 1.5px stroke in Deep Coastal Blue (`#173F56`) or Ocean Blue (`#397FA8`). Never filled or solid.
