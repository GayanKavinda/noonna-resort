Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your coastal retreat at Noonna Beach Hotel.

What room types are available at Noonna Beach Hotel? keyboard_arrow_down
We offer private beachfront coastal units featuring a plush king-size bed, organic cotton linens, a private en-suite open-air bathroom, and a spacious ocean-facing veranda. Extra bed arrangements for families can be coordinated upon request.
What are your check-in and check-out times? keyboard_arrow_down
Check-in is welcomed from 2:00 PM (14:00) onward, and check-out is until 11:00 AM. If you require early arrival or delayed departure to match your travel schedule, please let us know in advance and we will accommodate luggage storage with pleasure.
What activities and guest facilities are available? keyboard_arrow_down
Guests enjoy direct sandy footpaths to uncrowded surf breaks, private shaded verandas, high-speed Wi-Fi, daily housekeeping, garden lounging, and curated coastal excursions including coastal trail walks and visits to Dondra Head Lighthouse.
How close is the hotel to Kumbalgama center and the beach? keyboard_arrow_down
Noonna Beach Hotel is positioned right along the serene shoreline with immediate barefoot access to the sand and gentle ocean tidal breaks. Kumbalgama center and local artisan village markets are just a short 5 to 10-minute stroll or quick tuk-tuk ride away.
What are the dining, breakfast, and parking options? keyboard_arrow_down
A nourishing artisanal coastal breakfast is served daily featuring fresh tropical fruits, authentic Sri Lankan hoppers, Ceylon single-origin tea, and local preserves. Complimentary secure private parking is provided on premises. For dining, authentic local curries and fresh seafood can be prepared by arrangement or enjoyed at nearby coastal spots.
Is there a swimming pool, spa, or airport shuttle service? keyboard_arrow_down
As an intimate beachfront sanctuary, we focus on natural ocean tidal pools and pristine shoreline sea dips directly in front of the retreat. Personalized in-room or veranda Ayurvedic massages can be arranged with local practitioners, and private air-conditioned airport transfers via the Southern Expressway can be coordinated prior to arrival.
Are bathrooms private, and is high-speed Wi-Fi provided? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes, each accommodation features its own private, en-suite artisanal open-sky rain shower and natural stone bathroom with organic botanical toiletries. High-speed, seamless Wi-Fi is complimentary throughout all suites and outdoor living verandas.
