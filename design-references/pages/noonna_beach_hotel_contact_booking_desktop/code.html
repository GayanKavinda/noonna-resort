Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your coastal retreat at Noonna Beach Hotel.

We offer private beachfront coastal units featuring plush king-size beds dressed in organic cotton linens, private en-suite open-air bathrooms, and spacious ocean-facing verandas overlooking the surf breaks. Extra bed arrangements and quiet working nooks can be tailored upon advance request.
Standard check-in begins at 14:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Early check-in or relaxed late departure can happily be arranged depending on villa availability. If you arrive early, leave your luggage with our concierge, refresh with a fresh Ceylon coconut, and enjoy the beach right away.
Yes, fresh seasonal breakfasts are freshly prepared daily. Enjoy a vibrant spread of tropical fruits, freshly squeezed juices, artisanal Ceylon teas, and your choice of traditional Sri Lankan curries or classic continental morning dishes on your shaded veranda or by the shore.
Certainly. Private air-conditioned vehicle transfers can be seamlessly arranged directly from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) or Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport (HRI) directly to our gates, as well as local tuk-tuks to Kumbalgama train station, Weligama, and Galle Fort.
Noonna Beach Hotel sits right along a tranquil stretch of golden sand facing gentle breaker tides. You have direct footstep access to serene tidal wading pools, peaceful morning walks, and proximity to famous surf points like Midigama and Weligama just minutes down the coast.
Each suite features a private en-suite open-air garden rain shower complete with artisan botanical bath amenities and plush towels. Complimentary high-speed fiber Wi-Fi is accessible throughout all guest suites, verandas, and property grounds.
